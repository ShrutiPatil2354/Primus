Home Maintenance Safety - Sample Knowledge
===========================================

This fictional checklist provides basic home maintenance guidance. It does
not replace a licensed tradesperson or an inspection.

Before starting
----------------
1. Identify the system and read the equipment manufacturer's instructions.
2. Tell other occupants what work is being done and keep children and pets
   away from the work area.
3. Shut off power, gas, or water when the task requires it, and verify that
   the system is isolated before touching it.
4. Wear task-appropriate eye, hand, and breathing protection.
5. Keep a flashlight, phone, first-aid kit, and fire extinguisher accessible.

Routine schedule
----------------
- Test smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms monthly using the test button.
- Replace alarm batteries according to the manufacturer's instructions and
  replace the alarm when its stated service life ends.
- Inspect HVAC filters monthly and replace them on the equipment schedule or
  sooner when visibly blocked.
- Check under-sink pipes, appliance hoses, and the water-heater area monthly
  for leaks or corrosion.
- Clean dryer lint screens after every load and inspect the vent periodically.

Stop-work conditions
--------------------
Leave the area and call a qualified professional for exposed wiring, a gas
smell or suspected gas leak, structural movement, asbestos, major water
damage, fire damage, or any task requiring a permit that the worker does not
hold.

Boundary
--------
This document contains no model-specific repair instructions, wiring diagrams,
part numbers, building-code requirements, or contractor recommendations.
