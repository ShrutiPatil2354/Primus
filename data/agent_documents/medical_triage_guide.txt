Medical Triage Guide - Sample Knowledge
=======================================

This fictional guide is for educational triage only. It is not a diagnosis,
prescription, or substitute for a licensed clinician.

Emergency warning signs
-----------------------
Recommend emergency services immediately for severe difficulty breathing,
severe or crushing chest pain, sudden weakness or paralysis on one side,
new confusion, fainting, uncontrolled heavy bleeding, blue lips, a severe
allergic reaction with breathing or swelling symptoms, or rapidly worsening
condition. The person should not drive themselves if they may be seriously
unwell.

Same-day clinical review
------------------------
Recommend contacting a healthcare professional the same day for persistent
high fever, dehydration signs such as very little urine, repeated vomiting,
moderate breathing difficulty, new severe pain, or symptoms in a person who
is pregnant, very young, elderly, or immunocompromised.

General supportive guidance
---------------------------
For mild symptoms, rest, drink appropriate fluids, monitor changes, and use
only medicines already approved for the person by a healthcare professional.
If symptoms persist, worsen, or the person is worried, contact a clinician.

Boundary
--------
This guide contains no diagnosis criteria, medication doses, disease
predictions, or personal medical records. Do not diagnose from symptoms or
recommend prescription changes. Do not request unnecessary health details.
