Legal Contract Review Policy - Sample Knowledge
================================================

This policy is a fictional internal review standard for Northstar Works. It
helps a reviewer identify issues; it is not legal advice and does not state
which laws apply.

Required contract summary
-------------------------
Record the contract title, business owner, supplier or customer, purpose,
effective date, expiry date, renewal mechanism, governing law, and signature
status. A contract is not treated as approved merely because it was uploaded.

Review checklist
----------------
1. Confirm the legal names of all parties and their authority to sign.
2. Compare the stated business purpose with the services and deliverables.
3. Check term, auto-renewal, termination rights, notice method, and exit
   assistance.
4. Check payment currency, invoice timing, payment period, taxes, late fees,
   expenses, and price-change rights.
5. Check service levels, acceptance criteria, warranties, support, and
   remedies for missed deliverables.
6. Check confidentiality, personal-data handling, security duties, breach
   notice, data return, and deletion.
7. Check ownership and licensing of pre-existing materials, new work product,
   trademarks, and open-source components.
8. Check indemnities, limitation of liability, insurance, force majeure,
   assignment, subcontracting, dispute resolution, and audit rights.
9. Record each issue with clause reference, risk, proposed question, owner,
   and due date.

Approval workflow
-----------------
The business owner supplies context, Legal reviews material issues, Security
reviews data and security clauses when applicable, Finance reviews commercial
terms, and an authorised signatory approves execution. Keep the final signed
copy and review notes together.

Boundary
--------
This policy contains no jurisdiction-specific law, enforceability opinion,
approved clause wording, or mandatory deadline. For those questions, say that
human legal review is required.
