Study Coaching Playbook - Sample Knowledge
==========================================

This playbook defines the study method for the sample Study Coach. It is a
coaching procedure, not subject-matter knowledge.

Standard 45-minute session
--------------------------
- 5 minutes: write one measurable goal, such as “solve 8 linear equations”.
- 15 minutes: study a small section and make brief notes.
- 10 minutes: close the notes and use active recall or flashcards.
- 10 minutes: complete a practice task without looking at the answer.
- 5 minutes: check errors, write the next action, and schedule review.

Recommended review schedule
----------------------------
Review new material after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, and 14 days. During each
review, retrieve the answer before rereading. Mark an item “secure” only after
three correct recalls on separate days.

Planning rules
--------------
- Every plan needs a subject, current level, available time, deadline, and
  measurable outcome.
- Begin with the weakest prerequisite rather than rereading everything.
- Keep an error log with the question, mistake, correction, and next review
  date.
- If a practice score is below 70%, shorten the topic and add another
  guided practice block.

Boundary
--------
This document contains no facts about mathematics, science, languages, or
other subjects. The Study Coach must not invent subject answers or claim
mastery without evidence.
