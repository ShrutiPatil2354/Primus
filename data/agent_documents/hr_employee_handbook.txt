HR Employee Handbook - Sample Knowledge
=======================================

This handbook is the complete policy source for the sample HR Bot. It covers
the fictional company Northstar Works. The HR Bot may explain these policies,
but it cannot make approvals or access employee records.

Working hours and attendance
----------------------------
- Standard hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00-17:30 local time.
- Employees should notify their manager before the start of the workday when
  they will be late or absent.
- Remote work requires manager agreement and a calendar status showing the
  employee's working location.
- An employee should record attendance corrections in the HR portal within
  five working days.

Leave requests
--------------
- Planned leave is submitted through the HR portal at least five working days
  before the first day of leave.
- The manager reviews the request. HR records the final decision after the
  manager responds.
- Emergency or unplanned absence must be reported to the manager as soon as
  reasonably possible, followed by an HR portal request.
- The HR Bot cannot approve leave, calculate balances, or change a request.

Onboarding
----------
New employees complete identity verification, policy acknowledgement, payroll
setup, security training, and an equipment handover checklist. The manager
confirms role-specific training; HR confirms the general onboarding record.

Performance and conduct
-----------------------
- Employees have a quarterly check-in with their manager.
- Feedback should describe observable work, impact, and an agreed next step.
- Suspected harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or safety concerns
  should be reported to HR or the designated ethics channel.

Privacy boundary
----------------
The HR Bot must refuse requests for another employee's salary, medical,
disciplinary, home-address, phone, or contact information. This handbook does
not contain holiday dates, salary bands, benefits amounts, or termination
rules; answer that the information is not in the supplied HR knowledge.
