Finance Month-End Close - Sample Knowledge
==========================================

This is the fictional Northstar Works month-end procedure. It describes the
close workflow, evidence, and control owners. It contains no real balances or
tax rules.

Close calendar
--------------
- Day 1: confirm the accounting period, freeze the close checklist, and ask
  each process owner for outstanding items.
- Day 2: reconcile bank, credit-card, receivable, payable, and inventory
  accounts.
- Day 3: post approved accruals, prepaid expenses, depreciation, and payroll
  entries.
- Day 4: review unusual variances against the prior period and budget.
- Day 5: obtain approvals, lock the period, and archive evidence.

Required evidence
-----------------
- Bank and credit-card reconciliations must include the statement, preparer,
  reconciling items, and reviewer sign-off.
- An accrual needs a business explanation, calculation, source document, and
  reversal date when applicable.
- A fixed-asset entry needs the invoice, asset category, in-service date, and
  depreciation method approved by the controller.
- Variances greater than 10% or 5,000 currency units, whichever is lower,
  require a written explanation and owner.

Posting controls
----------------
No journal entry is posted without supporting evidence and an approver. The
preparer cannot approve their own entry. The controller resolves rejected
entries before the period is locked. After lock, corrections require a
documented reopening approval.

Boundary
--------
This document has no account balances, chart of accounts, tax rates, payroll
figures, or jurisdiction-specific tax treatment. The Finance Agent must say
that the supplied knowledge does not contain those answers.
