Travel Planning Preferences - Sample Knowledge
==============================================

This is a fictional planning profile for a sample two-person trip. It is
reference data, not live travel information.

Traveler profile
----------------
- Travelers: two adults.
- Preferred pace: relaxed, with no more than two major activities per day.
- Interests: local food, museums, walking neighborhoods, parks, and accessible
  public transport.
- Avoid: overnight travel, tightly packed schedules, extreme adventure, and
  restaurant reservations before 19:00.
- Budget target: moderate, up to 180 currency units per person per day,
  excluding international transport.

Planning rules
--------------
1. Collect destination, dates, travelers, budget, pace, interests, mobility
   needs, dietary needs, and passport or visa constraints.
2. Put confirmed bookings in a Confirmed section and suggestions in a
   Suggested section.
3. Keep transport, lodging, activities, meals, and emergency information in
   separate sections.
4. Leave at least 60 minutes between scheduled activities and include a rest
   period after a long travel segment.
5. Ask for missing constraints instead of assuming them.

Boundary
--------
This document contains no destination, hotel, flight, price, opening hour,
visa rule, weather forecast, or reservation. Never claim these details are
current without a verified official source.
